What happens after you buy an organisation licence

  1. You enter your tenant at checkout

    The checkout form asks for your Microsoft Entra tenant domain (for example contoso.com) or the tenant ID. We resolve it to your tenant's GUID using Microsoft's public discovery document. No sign-in, no permission in your tenant.

  2. Your licence row is written automatically

    When Stripe confirms payment, the licence is recorded against your tenant GUID: the tier, the term, and the Stripe subscription reference. That is the entire record. Your name, email and billing details stay in Stripe, where they have to live anyway; none of it is copied to us.

  3. Any admin activates it in the app

    In LAPSlock, Settings → Activate license. The app sends only your tenant GUID and receives a signed entitlement. Every admin who signs in from your tenant gets the same result. There are no licence keys to distribute and no accounts to create.

The app never carries a price, a checkout link or a "buy" button. Purchasing for organisations lives on this page and nowhere else. If you arrived here from the app, something is wrong and we would like to know.

Questions

Why is the licence keyed to a tenant instead of to users?

Because it is the only key that requires nothing from you. The app already knows which tenant it is signed into. Keying on that means no seats to count, no user list to send us, and no personal data on our side. A tenant GUID identifies an organisation, not a person.

We have 520 devices. Which Enterprise band?

Unlimited. The 500-device band is on the honour system: nothing in the app counts your devices, and nothing reports the count to us. If you outgrow it, email us and we will move you to unlimited for the difference.

What does the licence check actually send?

Your tenant GUID, roughly once a month, over HTTPS, to a single Azure Function that returns a signed tier. Not device names, not user names, not how often anyone revealed a password. The full request and response contract is published as ENTITLEMENT-API.md, and the how it works page explains how to verify it yourself.

Can we pay by invoice, purchase order or ACH?

Yes. Email connor@kainor.com with the tier and your tenant domain. You will receive a Stripe invoice that accepts ACH and card, with your PO number on it and net-30 if you need it. Vendor forms and security questionnaires are answered by the same address.

What if we cancel?

The licence runs to the end of the paid year and then the tenant returns to the free tier. Nothing is deleted, nobody is locked out of the app, and the reveal meter simply applies again.

Does the free tier really stay free?

Yes. Five reveals a month is the permanent floor, not a trial that expires. It exists so a sceptical admin can verify every claim we make before paying for anything.
